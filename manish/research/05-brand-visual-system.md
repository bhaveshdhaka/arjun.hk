# 05 · Brand & Visual System

Derived from vision-model analysis of 9 representative assets (competition reels, training footage, profile portrait) harvested 30 Aug 2026. Originals were **not** copied into the repo — this is the extracted system.

## Core finding: the brand is a duality

Two aesthetics coexist and alternate in his feed:

| Face | Palette | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **The Warrior** | combat red, ink black, overlay gold | competition reels, war-rounds photos, bag work |
| **The Healer** | Himalayan greens, mist grey, mat teal, soft whites | mountain training, nature movement, portrait |

A pain-free strength coach who is also a champion fighter — the website should hold both, not average them away.

## Typography

- **No formal brand fonts exist.** His overlay style: heavy condensed all-caps sans (Impact/Anton class), white or `#FFD520` fill, occasional thick dark stroke; secondary style is Instagram-story bold sans in Title Case.
- **Web direction:** display **Anton** or **Archivo Black** (all caps, tight leading) for headlines; **Inter** for body. Matches his overlay energy without shipping novelty fonts.

## Photography / motion style

- **Documentary candour over polish** — phone-shot, 9:16 vertical, available light, motion blur, lived-in gyms, rooftop mats, mountain ridges. His audience reads this as proof of authenticity; studio gloss would break the brand.
- Recurring compositions: low-angle action, subject centered, big negative space (mats/foreground) that his text overlays occupy — the same real-estate logic works for web hero images.
- Signature scenery: **training in the Himalayas** (monsoon mist, ridgelines) — instantly differentiating imagery for the About section.

## Logo & marks

- **None exists.** Profile image is a lifestyle portrait (black polo, café, contemplative), not a mark.
- Options for the site: (a) wordmark "SOUTHPAW AESTHETIC" in Anton all-caps — zero design risk, matches overlay culture; (b) commission a southpaw-stance glyph later. Do **not** invent a logo during the build without his sign-off.
