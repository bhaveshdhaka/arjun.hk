## Tone rules for ghostwritten copy

1. **First-person, plain-spoken, zero corporate gloss.** Short sentences. Verbs first.
2. **Gratitude is structural** — he credits students, mentors, team before himself.
3. **Devotional/fighter register coexists with rehab professionalism** — never sanitize the 🔱/Oss out entirely; it's his identity, and his audience signs along.
4. **Claims stay experiential** ("this is how you stay in the fight"), never medical ("cures", "treats", "guarantees").
5. **Punjabi-English rhythm** — "Blessed with good people", "Day well spent", trailing ellipses "….." are his cadence. Ghostwritten copy should feel spoken, not written.
6. **Emoji discipline:** 💪 🥋 ⚔️ 🔱 🙏 🥇 — this set only.
