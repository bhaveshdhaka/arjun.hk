The method

Slow is a weapon.

Built on the ATG progression framework: start from the ground up, train both short and long ranges, go light-to-heavy, slow-to-fast. Every session scales to where your body is today — that's how pain loses its grip and performance compounds. Your body will thank you in ten years for what you're doing today.

ATG Split SquatSingle-leg strength that exposes and fixes imbalances before they become injuries.
Nordic CurlHamstring armour — built progressively from zero.
Step-UpKnee ability you can actually use, on stairs and on the mat.
Sled WorkStrength with zero eccentric soreness. The base-builder.
Loaded Carries & DipsFull-range upper-body strength at optimal ranges of motion.
Tibialis & Calf WorkThe overlooked lower-leg bullets that keep you in the fight.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this physiotherapy or medical treatment?

No. This is strength & movement coaching built on the ATG framework — progressive, measurable training scaled to your current ability. It is not medical diagnosis or injury treatment.

Does online coaching actually work?

The 30-kilogram weighted pull-up story happened on two in-person sessions a week — everything else was homework and discipline over a shared plan. That's the model, and it's documented on my Instagram.

I'm not a fighter. Is this for me?

Fighters are my loudest clients, not my only ones. Corporate professionals, seniors, women, and anyone who wants to move without pain train here — the method scales from zero to champion.

What is ATG?

Athletic Truth Group — Ben Patrick's ("Knees Over Toes Guy") training system: build from the ground up, both short and long ranges, light to heavy, slow to fast. I'm an ATG Level 3 certified coach, listed in ATG's official directory.

Where are you based?

Mohali, Punjab — serving the Tricity in person and the world online. DM to start.
